Morning at the harbor

Saturday, Oct 10 · 412 words

The fog sat low over the water until almost nine. I walked the long way past the boatyard and counted the masts, the way I used to with Dad, and lost count somewhere after forty.

I keep meaning to write more about the small things. Today it was the gulls arguing over a single fry, and the woman at the café who remembered my order without asking.
